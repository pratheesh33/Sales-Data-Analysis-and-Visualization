# Sales Data Analysis and Visualization
### Tools: Microsoft Excel | Dataset: 500 Orders | Region: Tamil Nadu

## Overview
End-to-end sales analysis of a grocery retail business across Tamil Nadu 
covering 10 product categories, 30 cities, and 10 years (2015–2024).

## What I Analyzed
- Category-wise sales and profit performance
- City-wise profit distribution across Tamil Nadu
- Year-on-year sales growth trends
- Sub-category breakdown with margin analysis
- Top customers by revenue contribution

## Key Insights
- Identified top 3 revenue-generating categories
- Mapped high-profit vs high-discount cities
- Tracked year-wise growth with YoY % change
- Built an interactive KPI dashboard summarizing ₹ metrics
